# Module 4 - Agentic AI and Vibe Coding
---
## What to Expect

In this module, you will explore emerging approaches such as agentic AI and vibe coding. You will learn how AI agents work, how to build agent-based workflows using frameworks such as RAG and orchestration tools, and how to integrate AI agents with data sources and APIs. You will also experiment with modern AI-assisted coding tools and compare AI-driven development approaches with traditional software development methods.
