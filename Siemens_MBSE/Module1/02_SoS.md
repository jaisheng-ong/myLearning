# Systems amd Systems of Systems (SoS)

## MBSE 
* **MBSE** - An approach to System Engineering which emphasizes the use of models, simulations, and formal methods to design, analyze and validate complex systems.

### Within MBSE - SoS
* **SoS** - A concept that describes higher level of abstraction and complexity where multiple independent systems interact to provide larger, more comprehensive capability.

* **Purpose** of SoS in MBSE:
  * **Manage Complexity**: 
    * SoS enables engineers to break down complex problems into smaller, more manageable parts.
    * By addressing **interactions and dependencies** between sub-systems, MBSE can **facilitate** the design of large-scale systems with many interconnected components.
  
  * **Enhance Interoperability**:
    * SoS approaches help ensure differet components or sub-systems can work together seamlessly.
    * Extremely useful in scenarios where systems are developed by different organizations, or where legacy systems need ti be integrated with newer technologies.

  * **Enable Flexibility and Scalability**:
    * Designing systems with SoS perspective allow easier adaptation to changes in requirements, technology or the environment.
    * Due to modular nature of SoS makes it simpler to add, remove or modify components without compromising the overall system's functionality.

  * **Facilitate Reuse and Standardization**:
    * Identifying common functions and interfaces, MBSE can promote reuse components and standardization of processes across different systems.
    * Result: Leads to reduced development costs and faster deployment of new capabilities.

  * **Support Decision Making**:
    * Use of models and simulations in MBSE enables better understanding of the behavior and performance of the overall system under various condition.
    * Results: help stakeholders make informed decisions about trade-offs, risks, and resource allocation.
